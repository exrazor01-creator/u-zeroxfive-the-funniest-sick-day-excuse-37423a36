## The entry

> Won't be in today - my dentist says I can't talk to anyone until the numbness wears off, and I'm not even mad about it.

119 characters, one line, plain ASCII — inside the 1–200 character gate.

## Why this line

Written against the judge's brief, in the order it weighs things:

1. **Half believable, fully funny** — a dentist visit with numbness is a real excuse a boss can't argue with, and a coworker would laugh at it.
2. **Fresh idea** — no fever, no dog, no dead grandmother: the excuse is a mouth that legally cannot talk.
3. **Twist near the end** — "I'm not even mad about it" lands last, quietly admitting the day off was the point. A second edit cut the filler "honestly" and added "even", so the admission lands harder as the final phrase.
4. **Sounds like a real person texting** — "Won't be in today" reads like a message, not a contest entry.
5. **No wasted words** — 119 characters, every part of the line doing work.
